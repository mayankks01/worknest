# WorkNest delivery agreement

- Implement one approved build step at a time.
- Finish the step, run its relevant checks, and update its verification notes.
- Commit and push each completed step to `origin` (https://github.com/mayankks01/worknest).
- Verify the remote received the commit. Never force-push over unrelated work.
- Report the result and ask for approval before starting the next step.
- Keep local environment secrets, dependencies, generated builds, and test credentials out of Git.
- Follow the supplied WorkNest mockup's navy/blue design; keep sample data clearly separate from real account and tenant data.

This agreement records the user's instruction on 11 October 2026 to push after every completed step.
